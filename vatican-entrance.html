Architectural Floorplan & Walking Route
Follow steps ❶ → ❷ → ❸ → ❹ into Cappella del Coro
1
Drop-off: Piazza del Sant'Uffizio

Tell your taxi or chauffeur: "Piazza del Sant'Uffizio (Vaticano)". Drop-off is right at the gate. Do not go into the main square tourist lines.

2
Gate: Cancello Petriano (15:00 Sharp)

Arrive by 14:45 – 15:00. Vatican security strictly shuts the gate at 15:00. Anyone arriving after 15:00 cannot be admitted.

3
Security Clearance & Escort

Swiss Guards verify your name against our guest list. Present your physical invitation card + passport/ID. Airport-style security check.

4
Cappella del Coro & Shuttles

Ushers seat you for the 15:30 blessing. At 16:45, private shuttles will wait right at Petriano Gate to take everyone to Villa Aurelia for aperitivo!

Wedding Day Dress Code & Vatican Modesty Protocol

Gentlemen: Black Tie — Classic black tuxedo with black bow tie, white formal shirt, and polished dress shoes.
Ladies: Floor-length gala evening gowns in delicate pastel and light colors (powder blue, blush pink, soft lavender, butter yellow, or champagne).

*VATICAN CHAPEL REQUIREMENT (MANDATORY): Inside St. Peter's Basilica, shoulders and knees must be covered. Gowns with sleeves, elegant capelet silhouettes, or matching silk shawls/wraps are strictly required for entry.
