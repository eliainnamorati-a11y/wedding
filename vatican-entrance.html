Cappella del Coro · Basilica di San Pietro in Vaticano

Saturday, April 17, 2027 • Ceremony: 15:30 • Doors Close: 15:00 Sharp
Strict Vatican Security Protocol — Arrival by 15:00 Sharp

Due to sovereign Vatican state regulations and security checks, all guests must arrive at the security gate by 15:00 at the very latest to clear security screening and be seated in the chapel. If you arrive after 15:00, access will strictly be denied by Vatican security with no exceptions. Please plan ample transit time.

01

Arrive at Piazza del Sant'Uffizio by 15:00 Sharp

Do not queue in the general public tourist lines in Piazza San Pietro. Instead, navigate to Piazza del Sant'Uffizio (located just south of the left colonnade, opposite the Paul VI Hall).

Taxis and private cars can drop guests off directly in front of the Cancello Petriano gate.

02

Vatican Security Credentials Check

At Cancello Petriano, the Swiss Guards and Vatican Gendarmerie manage access strictly according to our submitted guest list:

  • Physical Invitation: Please present your formal printed wedding invitation suite or wedding pass card.
  • Photo ID: A valid passport or European national ID card is strictly mandatory.
  • Security Screening: Airport-style bag scanning and metal detector walk-through. Sharp items, glass containers, and large suitcases or backpacks are strictly prohibited.
03

Escort to Cappella del Coro & Blessing

Once cleared, ushers will guide you through the reserved southern entrance of St. Peter's Basilica into the stunning, gilded Cappella del Coro.

The ceremony begins punctually at 15:30. Once inside, you will experience the organ and solemn choir blessing in one of the most sacred and historic chapels in the Christian world.

04

Shuttle Transport to Villa Aurelia (17:30 Aperitivo)

Following the blessing and photos, dedicated private shuttles will be stationed outside the gate to transfer all wedding guests directly up the Janiculum Hill to Villa Aurelia.

The garden aperitivo begins at 17:30, followed by seated gala dinner and dancing under the stars until late.

Dress Code & Vatican Modesty Protocol

Gentlemen: Black Tie — Classic black tuxedo with black silk bow tie, white formal shirt, and polished dress shoes.
Ladies: Floor-length gala evening gowns in delicate pastel and light colors.

*MANDATORY VATICAN CHAPEL RULE: Inside St. Peter's Basilica, shoulders and knees must be respectfully covered. Gowns with sleeves, elegant capelet silhouettes, or matching silk shawls/wraps are strictly required for entry.
