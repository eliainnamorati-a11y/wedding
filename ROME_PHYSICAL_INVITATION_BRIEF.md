## 1. SUITE OVERVIEW & PRINT PHILOSOPHY

### Aesthetic Direction
* **Mood:** Regal Italian elegance, Old-World European romance, Vatican solemnity, haute couture editorial.
* **Inspiration:** Handcrafted Italian paper (Amalfi / Fabriano style), antique Roman gold foil, crisp letterpress impression, hand-calligraphed script, and architectural motifs.
* **Brand Monogram:** Custom intertwined **E&D** calligraphy monogram (ideal for blind debossing or gold foil stamping on envelope flaps, wax seals, or card headers).

### Suggested Production & Paper Specifications
* **Paper Stock:** Heavyweight handmade cotton paper (approx. 500 – 600 gsm / duplexed) with optional deckled or hand-gilded gold beveled edges.
* **Printing Methods:** 
  * Letterpress (deep blind deboss or warm charcoal ink).
  * Hot foil stamping in antique satin gold (not overly reflective yellow gold).
* **Color Palette:**
  * **Primary Paper:** Warm Parchment Ivory / Soft Travertine White.
  * **Ink:** Deep Charcoal Black / Dark Espresso Ink.
  * **Metallic Accent:** Antique Roman Matte Gold (`#BFA175`).
  * **Envelope / Accent Paper:** Warm Neutral Sand, Faded Olive, or Blush Silk.
* **Enclosure / Presentation:** Silk velvet ribbon, vellum wrap, or personalized wax seal bearing the **E&D** monogram.
